The technology

How an air-to-air heat pump works

Modern air conditioning systems are heat pumps. Understanding the technology explains why they’re so much more efficient than any other form of electric heating or cooling.

3–5×
more energy delivered than electricity consumed (COP 3–5)

2–in–1
same system heats in winter and cools in summer

Cooling mode, summer

The indoor unit draws warm room air across a cold refrigerant coil, absorbing the heat. A compressor raises the pressure and temperature of the refrigerant, then pumps it to the outdoor unit where a fan disperses the heat outside. The cooled refrigerant cycles back and your room air returns noticeably cooler.

Heating mode, winter

The cycle runs in reverse. Even at −15°C there is usable heat energy in outside air. The outdoor unit extracts this latent heat into the refrigerant, the compressor concentrates it, and the indoor unit delivers warm air inside, producing 3–5 units of heat for every unit of electricity consumed.

Why the efficiency numbers are so impressive

A gas boiler burns fuel to create heat, converting roughly 90p of every £1 of gas into warmth. An electric panel heater converts electricity to heat at a flat 1:1 ratio. A heat pump doesn’t create heat, it moves it. Shifting energy is far cheaper than generating it, which is why heat pumps deliver three to five times more energy as heat or cooling than they consume as electricity.

  • Air filtration: built-in filters capture dust, pollen and mould spores, improving indoor air quality throughout the year
  • Dehumidification: the cooling cycle removes moisture from the air, reducing condensation and damp in kitchens, bathrooms and bedrooms
  • Lower carbon: no direct combustion at the point of use. As the grid gets greener, so does your heating and cooling
  • Precise control: room-by-room temperature settings via remote, app or smart home integration

Is it right for your home? Air-to-air heat pumps work best in well-insulated spaces and suit newer builds or retrofitted properties particularly well. They work alongside existing heating rather than replacing it overnight. We always carry out a free survey first so you know exactly what to expect before spending a penny.

FAQ

Questions we're asked a lot

Does one unit really both heat and cool?

Yes. A modern air conditioning system is a heat pump. In summer it moves heat out of your home, and in winter it reverses to bring warmth in from the outside air, even on cold days. Because it moves heat rather than creating it, it's typically three to five times more efficient than a standard electric heater.

How much does installation cost?

Every property is different, so we quote after a free survey. As a rough guide, a single-room wall-mounted system starts from around £1,200–£1,800 supplied and fitted. Your quote is fixed with no surprises on the invoice.

How long does installation take?

Most single-unit installations are completed in a day. Multi-room or commercial systems typically take one to two days depending on the layout.

How often should my system be serviced?

Once a year keeps a system efficient, healthy and within warranty terms. We offer annual service plans with reminders, so you never have to think about it.
